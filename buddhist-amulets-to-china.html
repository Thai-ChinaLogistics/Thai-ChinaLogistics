泰国佛牌、佛像和供奉用品寄中国需要注意什么

佛牌、佛像、佛珠、佛牌盒、供奉用品及相关宗教工艺品可能易碎、价值较高或材质特殊，发货前需要先确认物品照片、尺寸、数量、材质、年代、来源和包装情况。较老、高价值或可能涉及特殊规定的物品，应先审核后再确认是否可寄和适合的运输方式。

寄件重点

佛牌、佛珠与配件

普通小件也应提供照片、尺寸、数量、材质和大致价值，便于确认线路与包装。

佛像与易碎供奉用品

根据尺寸、材质和易碎程度安排缓冲、固定、边角保护和外箱加固。

年代与来源审核

年代久远、高价值或可能涉及文物/特殊规定的物品，应提前说明来源与情况并确认是否可运输。

怎么开始

1. 提供货物信息发送货物图片、品类、数量、重量与尺寸。
2. 提供路线信息说明泰国取件地址和中国最终目的地。
3. 确认可寄与报价审核后确认运输方式、费用、包装和预计时效。

常见问题

佛牌、佛珠和佛牌盒可以一起寄吗？

普通商品类物品可以先按一批提供照片、材质、数量和价值信息，确认包装和线路后再安排。

佛像和易碎供奉用品怎么防止运输损坏？

需要根据材质、尺寸和易碎程度选择缓冲、固定、边角保护和外箱加固。

寺庙或商店购买的宗教用品可以代取后寄中国吗？

可先提供取件地址、联系人、商品信息和购买凭证，确认当地取件及货物审核后再安排转寄。

老物件或高价值佛牌怎么办？

应提前说明年代、价值和来源情况，必要时先确认相关规定；不能按普通新商品直接处理。

What to check before shipping Thai amulets and Buddhist items to China

Thai amulets, Buddha statues, prayer beads, amulet cases, altar items and related religious craft goods may be fragile, valuable or made from unusual materials. Photos, dimensions, quantity, material, age, provenance and packing should be reviewed first, especially for older, high-value or potentially regulated items.

Shipping priorities

Identify the item

Provide front/back photos, dimensions, quantity, material and approximate value.

Protective packing

Boxes, cushioning, corner protection and outer-carton reinforcement depend on the object.

Special-item review

Older, high-value or potentially regulated objects should be checked before shipment is accepted.

How to start

1. Share cargo detailsSend photos, category, quantity, weight and dimensions.
2. Share route detailsProvide the pickup address in Thailand and final destination in China.
3. Confirm acceptance and quoteThe cargo is reviewed before transport mode, price, packing and estimated transit are confirmed.

Frequently asked questions

Can Thai amulets be shipped to China?

Commercial-type amulets can be reviewed from photos and item details; final acceptance follows current rules.

How are statues protected from damage?

Packing is selected according to material, size, weight and fragility, with cushioning and reinforcement as needed.

What about old or high-value amulets?

Disclose age, value and provenance where relevant so any special requirements can be checked first.
