清迈寄中国可以提供哪些服务

从清迈寄货到中国，可根据货物类型和地址安排上门取件、酒店或公寓取件、商场/门店代取、空运、陆运及中国末端派送。个人行李、手工艺品、搬家物品和商业补货都可先提供照片、重量、尺寸与目的地，由客服确认可寄线路和报价。

寄件重点

清迈上门取件

酒店、公寓、住宅、商场或仓库等地址可按具体位置咨询。

空运与陆运

急件优先比较空运；大件、批量或时间较宽松的货物可比较陆运。

中国目的地派送

报价前确认收件城市、货物信息和是否需要包装或其他增值服务。

怎么开始

1. 提供货物信息发送货物图片、品类、数量、重量与尺寸。
2. 提供路线信息说明泰国取件地址和中国最终目的地。
3. 确认可寄与报价审核后确认运输方式、费用、包装和预计时效。

常见问题

清迈寄中国多少钱、要多久？

价格和时效由重量/体积、货物类型、清迈取件地址、中国目的地以及运输方式共同决定。手工艺品、大件或多箱货需要先提供材质、照片和尺寸再比较方案。

清迈寄中国怎么开始？

提供取件地址、中国目的地、货物图片、品类、重量和尺寸，先确认是否可寄和报价。

清迈酒店、公寓或商店可以代取后寄中国吗？

可以先提供地址、联系人、取件凭证和物品信息；酒店遗留物品可参考行李与酒店物品转寄，门店代取可参考代购跑腿与转运。

清迈手工艺品或搬家物品可以发中国吗？

可以先提供材质、尺寸、数量、照片和大致价值；大件或多箱货可同时比较陆运方案。

哪些货物需要提前审核？

食品、液体、粉末、电子产品、特殊工艺品等建议发货前先提供图片和详细信息。

Shipping services from Chiang Mai to China

Ship from Chiang Mai to China with pickup from homes, hotels, condos, shops or other agreed locations, followed by air or land freight and delivery in China. Personal luggage, handicrafts, moving goods and commercial restocking can be reviewed from photos, weight, dimensions and destination before a route and quote are confirmed.

Shipping priorities

Pickup in Chiang Mai

Hotel, condo, home, shop or warehouse pickup can be discussed using the exact address.

Air or land freight

Air is considered for urgent cargo; land freight is considered for bulkier or less time-sensitive shipments.

Delivery in China

The destination city and cargo details are confirmed before a final shipping plan and quote.

How to start

1. Share cargo detailsSend photos, category, quantity, weight and dimensions.
2. Share route detailsProvide the pickup address in Thailand and final destination in China.
3. Confirm acceptance and quoteThe cargo is reviewed before transport mode, price, packing and estimated transit are confirmed.

Frequently asked questions

How do I ship from Chiang Mai to China?

Send the pickup address, destination city, cargo photos, category, weight and dimensions for review.

Can you collect from a Chiang Mai hotel, condo or store?

Share the address, contact person, pickup proof and item details. Hotel-left items fit the luggage forwarding page; store collection fits the errand and forwarding page.

Can Chiang Mai handicrafts or moving goods be shipped to China?

Share material, dimensions, quantity, photos and approximate value. Bulky or multi-carton cargo can also be compared with land freight.

Which items require review first?

Food, liquids, powders, electronics and other special items should be checked before shipment.
