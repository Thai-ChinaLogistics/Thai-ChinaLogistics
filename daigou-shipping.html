寄件重点

跑腿代取与门店取货

已购买商品、门店自提件或需要从指定地址代取的物品，可先提供取件地点、联系人和物品信息确认。

代收、集货与合箱

多家供应商或多笔订单可以先集中，再按货物属性确认合箱、分箱、拍照或简单核对。

转寄中国与长期发货

适合游客临时转寄、代购团队、小批量多批次、电商补货和长期合作客户。

怎么开始

1. 提供货物信息发送货物图片、品类、数量、重量与尺寸。
2. 提供路线信息说明泰国取件地址和中国最终目的地。
3. 确认可寄与报价审核后确认运输方式、费用、包装和预计时效。

常见问题

可以帮忙跑腿去商场、门店或指定地址取东西吗？

可以先提供准确地址、联系人、取件凭证、物品图片和预计取件时间，确认当地安排后再执行。

可以帮忙代收多个订单并合箱吗？

可以咨询按批次代收、集货和合箱，具体仓储、核对与操作规则需提前确认。

取到东西以后可以直接转寄中国吗？

可以在货物审核通过后衔接包装和跨境运输；特殊品类需要先确认是否可寄。

长期代购团队怎么合作？

建议明确预计频率、平均货量、货物类型和对账需求后制定固定流程。

Errand pickup & store collection

For prepaid goods or items waiting at a store or specified address, share the exact pickup point, contact person and item details first.

Receiving, consolidation & repacking

Orders from multiple suppliers can be collected, checked, consolidated or split before dispatch.

Forwarding and recurring shipments

Suitable for travelers, online sellers, purchasing teams and customers shipping small batches frequently.

How to start

1. Share cargo detailsSend photos, category, quantity, weight and dimensions.
2. Share route detailsProvide the pickup address in Thailand and final destination in China.
3. Confirm acceptance and quoteThe cargo is reviewed before transport mode, price, packing and estimated transit are confirmed.

Frequently asked questions

Can you collect an item from a store or specified address?

Share the exact address, contact person, pickup proof, item photos and preferred timing so local pickup can be confirmed first.

Can you receive and consolidate multiple supplier orders?

Receiving, consolidation and repacking can be discussed by batch; storage and handling rules are confirmed in advance.

Can collected goods be forwarded directly to China?

Yes after cargo review. Restricted or special categories must be confirmed before cross-border shipment.

How do long-term purchasing teams cooperate?

Share expected frequency, typical volume, cargo categories and reconciliation needs so a stable process can be designed.

รับของแล้วส่งต่อไปจีนได้หรือไม่?

ลูกค้าระยะยาวควรเตรียมข้อมูลอะไร?
