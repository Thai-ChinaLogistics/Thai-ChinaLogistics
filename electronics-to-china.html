泰国电子产品寄中国需要确认什么

手机、电脑、平板、相机、游戏机、智能手表及其他带电设备寄往中国前，需要先确认品牌型号、电池类型与数量、设备状态、货值和包装情况。不同设备和电池配置可走的线路可能不同，先审核货物信息再安排运输更稳妥。

寄件重点

设备与电池信息

手机、电脑、相机、游戏机等需确认品牌型号、内置还是独立电池、数量以及设备状态。

高价值与防护包装

屏幕、镜头、边角和易损部位应根据设备价值和运输方式加强保护，必要时使用原包装或加固外箱。

门店/酒店代取后转寄

已购买或遗留在泰国的电子设备，可先提供取件地址、凭证和设备信息，确认当地取件及运输线路。

怎么开始

1. 提供货物信息发送货物图片、品类、数量、重量与尺寸。
2. 提供路线信息说明泰国取件地址和中国最终目的地。
3. 确认可寄与报价审核后确认运输方式、费用、包装和预计时效。

常见问题

手机、电脑、相机或游戏机可以从泰国寄中国吗？

可以先提供品牌型号、数量、电池情况、货值和图片，由客服按当前线路审核。

高价值电子设备怎么包装？

建议优先使用原包装或充分的防震材料，并保护屏幕、镜头、边角和接口，必要时加固外箱。

酒店遗留的手机或电脑可以帮忙取出来寄回中国吗？

可先提供酒店地址、联系人、取件授权/凭证和设备信息，确认当地取件与带电货物线路后再安排。

备用电池能一起寄吗？

独立电池通常要求更严格，必须在发货前单独说明和确认。

What to check before shipping electronics to China

Phones, laptops, tablets, cameras, game consoles, smart watches and other battery-powered devices should be reviewed for model, battery type and quantity, condition, declared value and packing before shipment. Available routes can differ by device and battery configuration, so cargo approval comes before the final transport plan.

Shipping priorities

Battery details

Confirm whether batteries are built in or separate, their quantity and the condition of the devices.

Protective packing

Screens, corners and fragile areas should be protected according to the value and transport mode.

Route review

Different devices and battery configurations may face different transport restrictions, so review comes first.

How to start

1. Share cargo detailsSend photos, category, quantity, weight and dimensions.
2. Share route detailsProvide the pickup address in Thailand and final destination in China.
3. Confirm acceptance and quoteThe cargo is reviewed before transport mode, price, packing and estimated transit are confirmed.

Frequently asked questions

Can phones be shipped from Thailand to China?

Send the model, quantity, battery information and photos for review against the current route.

How should laptops be packed?

Use cushioning to protect the screen and corners and reinforce the outer carton when needed.

Can spare batteries be included?

Separate batteries usually face stricter rules and must be declared and approved before shipment.
