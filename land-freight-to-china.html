A reference range is about 7–15 days, but customs, holidays, route conditions and destination can change the actual transit time.

Can personal luggage use land freight?

Yes, depending on weight, volume and how quickly the luggage is needed.

Can businesses ship regularly?

Yes. Recurring and multi-batch commercial shipments can be discussed.
