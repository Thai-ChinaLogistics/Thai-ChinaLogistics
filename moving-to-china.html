从泰国搬家回中国怎么安排

个人物品、家具、家电和其他大件从泰国搬回中国时，建议先按衣物、书籍、家居用品、电子产品、家具等分类并整理大致清单。根据箱数、尺寸、重量、是否可拆装、取件城市和中国目的地，再分别比较空运、陆运或其他可行方案。

寄件重点

物品清单

先按衣物、书籍、家居用品、电子产品、家具和大件物品分类，特殊物品单独标注。

家具与大件评估

桌椅、柜子、床架、电器等需要按尺寸、重量、材质、是否可拆装和包装条件单独评估。

分批运输与上门

急用物品和大件物品可根据时间与成本采用不同方案，并结合泰国取件地址和中国目的地安排。

怎么开始

1. 提供货物信息发送货物图片、品类、数量、重量与尺寸。
2. 提供路线信息说明泰国取件地址和中国最终目的地。
3. 确认可寄与报价审核后确认运输方式、费用、包装和预计时效。

常见问题

泰国搬家回中国多少钱？家具和大件怎么报价？

搬家没有统一按公斤固定价。报价通常需要箱数、主要物品、家具/家电尺寸重量、是否可拆装、泰国取件地址、中国目的地以及是否需要上门和末端派送；家具、大件最好单独拍照测量。

搬家回国要先准备什么？

建议提供城市、箱数、主要物品、预计重量尺寸和中国收货地址；家具和家电最好单独拍照并提供尺寸。

家具、床垫、桌椅和家电能不能寄？

需要根据尺寸、重量、材质、是否可拆装、是否带电及当期运输条件单独确认，不建议未审核就直接打包发货。

急用小件和家具大件可以分开安排运输吗？

可以。急用的小件和家具、大件可根据时效、体积和预算分别比较运输方案，再统一确认泰国取件与中国目的地派送安排。

个人物品需要清关资料吗？

不同货物和清关方式要求不同，发货前会根据实际情况确认。

How to arrange a move from Thailand to China

For personal effects, household goods, furniture and bulky items, start with a simple inventory covering the main categories and approximate carton count. Dimensions, weight, disassembly, pickup city and destination in China are then used to compare air, land or other available transport options.

Shipping priorities

Prepare an inventory

Group clothing, books, household goods, electronics, furniture and other bulky items, and flag anything special.

Furniture & bulky-item review

Tables, chairs, cabinets, bed frames and appliances need separate review for dimensions, weight, material, disassembly and packing.

Split by urgency and route

Urgent personal effects and bulky cargo can use different transport plans based on the Thai pickup address and final city in China.

How to start

1. Share cargo detailsSend photos, category, quantity, weight and dimensions.
2. Share route detailsProvide the pickup address in Thailand and final destination in China.
3. Confirm acceptance and quoteThe cargo is reviewed before transport mode, price, packing and estimated transit are confirmed.

Frequently asked questions

What information is needed for a moving quote?

Provide the Thai city, number of cartons, main contents, approximate weight/dimensions and destination in China. Furniture and appliances should be photographed and measured separately.

Can furniture, mattresses, tables or appliances be shipped?

They require separate review for dimensions, weight, material, disassembly, battery/electrical issues and current transport conditions.

Can urgent cartons and bulky items use different transport options?

Yes. Urgent cartons and bulky furniture can be compared separately by transit time, volume and budget, then coordinated under the same Thailand pickup and China delivery plan.

Are customs documents required for personal effects?

Requirements vary by cargo and clearance method and are confirmed before shipment.
