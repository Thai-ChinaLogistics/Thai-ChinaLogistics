寄件重点

物品清单

先按衣物、书籍、家居用品、电子产品等分类，特殊物品单独标注。

分批运输

急用物品和大件物品可以根据时间与成本考虑不同运输方式。

上门与目的地

结合泰国出发地址和中国最终收货城市安排取件与派送。

怎么开始

1. 提供货物信息发送货物图片、品类、数量、重量与尺寸。
2. 提供路线信息说明泰国取件地址和中国最终目的地。
3. 确认可寄与报价审核后确认运输方式、费用、包装和预计时效。

常见问题

搬家回国要先准备什么？

建议提供城市、箱数、主要物品、预计重量尺寸和中国收货地址。

家具和大件能不能寄？

需要根据尺寸、重量、材质和当期运输条件单独确认。

个人物品需要清关资料吗？

不同货物和清关方式要求不同，发货前会根据实际情况确认。

Group clothing, books, household goods, electronics and other items, and flag anything special.

Split by urgency

Urgent personal effects and bulky cartons can use different transport plans when appropriate.

Pickup and final delivery

The plan is based on the Thailand pickup address and the final receiving city in China.

1. Share cargo detailsSend photos, category, quantity, weight and dimensions.

Frequently asked questions

What information is needed for a moving quote?

Provide the Thai city, number of cartons, main contents, approximate weight/dimensions and destination in China.

Can furniture or oversized items be shipped?

They need separate review based on dimensions, weight, material and current transport conditions.

Are customs documents required for personal effects?

Requirements vary by cargo and clearance method and are confirmed before shipment.
